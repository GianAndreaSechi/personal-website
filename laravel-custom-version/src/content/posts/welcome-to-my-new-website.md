Welcome. I’m Gian Andrea, a software engineer based in Italy, currently working at Musixmatch.

I wanted this website to bring together things that otherwise live in different places: my open-source projects, older experiments, a bit of my story and some room to write.

## Projects from different chapters

You’ll find [irides](/projects/irides), which I’m building to make database structures accessible to applications and AI tools. You’ll also find [NearMe](/projects/near-me) and [NearMe Data](/projects/near-me-data), projects from the pandemic, alongside smaller utilities and my [school project on numerical integration](/projects/dynamical-systems-numerical-integration).

They aren’t all at the same stage. Some are ongoing, some are concluded and others remain experiments. I’ve kept them here because they are part of how I got here.

## A little more than work

The [About page](/about-me) includes my path through software, mathematics and the humanities. My diploma with honours (100 e lode) is there too: it still matters to me, even after years of working in software.

For the career details, there’s a separate [experience and CV page](/resume).

## A space to write

I’ll use the blog for occasional notes about software, projects, things I’m learning and ideas I want to think through. There’s no publication schedule; I’d rather write when I have something to share.

If something here interests you, or you want to ask about a project, [say hello](/contact).
