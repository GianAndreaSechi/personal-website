## Why I keep a personal website

I want somewhere to bring together what I build, what I’m interested in and what I learn along the way. LinkedIn and my CV describe my career; here I can give projects and ideas more room.

## How it has evolved

Earlier versions used PHP, MySQL, Bootstrap and jQuery. The current application uses Laravel, Tailwind CSS and Vite. Keeping the older projects here is part of documenting that evolution.

## Writing and maintenance

The blog supports Markdown files versioned in Git as well as editing through an administration area. Project descriptions are also stored as Markdown, with a command to sync them into the database. A dynamic sitemap reflects the public pages and published articles.

These choices let me maintain the site while keeping the writing alongside the code. There will be occasional notes and longer articles as I find things worth sharing.
