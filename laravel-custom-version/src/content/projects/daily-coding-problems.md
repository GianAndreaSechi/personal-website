## Practising with small problems

This repository collects my solutions to several Daily Coding Problem exercises. They cover tasks such as finding a pair with a given sum, transforming arrays and serializing a binary tree.

## Why keep them here

These are exercises rather than applications. They document another side of learning to program: working through a small problem, choosing a representation and looking at the constraints.

The questions come from Daily Coding Problem; my contribution is the solutions. Company names mentioned in the questions are not claims of work or collaboration with those companies.
