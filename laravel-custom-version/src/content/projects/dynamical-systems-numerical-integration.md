## Where it started

For my final school project at I.T.I.S. Primo Levi, I explored continuous-time dynamical systems and numerical integration. It brought together two interests I still enjoy: mathematics and programming.

## The experiment

I implemented the Lorenz 1963 model in C using three integration methods: Euler, second-order Runge–Kutta and fourth-order Runge–Kutta. The project compares the resulting trajectories and discusses how they diverge over time.

It was a way to see how an algorithm affects a simulation, rather than treating a numerical method as just a formula on paper.

## Why it is still here

I graduated in 2009 with 100/100 with honours (100 e lode) and was included in the Italian National Register of Excellence. This work belongs to that chapter of my education, and I’m glad to keep it alongside more recent projects.

The repository contains the original Italian write-up and C implementations. It is a school project, not a peer-reviewed research publication.
