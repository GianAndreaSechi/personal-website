## Why we started it

During the first COVID-19 lockdowns, finding out which local shops were open and how they were operating was often difficult. Small businesses also needed a way to communicate changes without having to build their own website.

I co-founded NearMe with Davide Zaccaria as a free, non-profit directory for local businesses, initially in the Modena area.

## What it offered

Businesses could share opening hours, delivery and takeaway options, and contact details. Residents could look for nearby services in one place.

## My part

I developed the website infrastructure and worked on copywriting and SEO. It was a practical application of the web development skills I had built at work, in response to a need close to home.

## Current state

NearMe closed in May 2021. This page preserves the project’s story rather than linking to a service that is still operating.

The initiative was covered by ModenaToday; my LinkedIn profile includes the article and further context.
